Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device and in your own private iCloud. Nothing is sent to us — there's no account and no server of ours.

How do I sync across my devices?
Sign each iPhone into the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled. Taskonaut syncs automatically through your private iCloud.

My tasks aren't syncing.
Check iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud and make sure iCloud is enabled and the device has a connection. Sync happens in the background and can take a moment.

How do I capture a task quickly?
Use the ➕ in the app, ask Siri, share text or a link from another app via the Share sheet, or tap the Home Screen widget. Everything lands in your Inbox to triage later.

How do I delete my data?
Delete individual tasks inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data. Changes sync to your other devices through iCloud.
