Privacy Policy

Taskonaut · Last updated 8 June 2026

Taskonaut is a personal task manager. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and your data stays yours.

Summary

Taskonaut has no account to create and no servers of ours. Your tasks are stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud, so the developer never receives them. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What the app stores

The tasks, notes, projects, tags, due dates, and time sessions you create are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). This lets your data sync across the iPhones signed into your Apple ID. There is no other copy and no backend of ours.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Data kept in your private iCloud database is accessible only to you, under your Apple ID — not to the developer. The developer cannot see your tasks or any other content you enter.

iCloud

Syncing is provided by Apple's iCloud/CloudKit and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings; the app will then keep your data on the device only.

Data we collect about you: none

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual tasks inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device; deletions you make in the app also sync to your other devices through iCloud, removing the synced copy.

Children

Taskonaut is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new "last updated" date.
